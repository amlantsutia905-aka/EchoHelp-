📖 Offline Emergency Guide

These basic safety reminders are available even when AI is offline.

❤️ Unresponsive person
Call emergency services. Check breathing. Follow the emergency dispatcher's instructions. Start CPR if trained or guided to do so.
🔥 Fire
Leave the building if safe. Avoid elevators. Stay low if there is smoke. Never re-enter a burning building.
🚶 Personal safety
Move toward a populated, well-lit place if possible. Contact a trusted person or emergency services.
🚗 Road accident
Keep yourself safe. Call emergency services. Do not move an injured person unless there is immediate danger.
📍 Lost or stranded
Stay in a safe public location. Share your location with someone you trust.
